Long histories.

Six starting points for exploring the museum, its collection, and the place around it. Each fact links back to the museum’s published information.

01 / An unusually long history.

America’s oldest car collection

The museum uses this description for the Anderson collection, begun in 1899.

Museum source ↗
02 / A collection within a collection.

32 purchased. 14 remain.

The Andersons bought 32 new cars during their lives; 14 survive in the permanent collection.

Museum source ↗
03 / Before the museum.

Tours by 1927

The Andersons opened their carriage house for collection tours before the museum’s October 1949 opening.

Museum source ↗
04 / Architecture with a past.

An 1888 carriage house

Architect Edmond March Wheelwright drew inspiration from France’s Château de Chaumont.

Museum source ↗
05 / Room to explore.

A 64-acre park

The museum sits within Larz Anderson Park in Brookline, Massachusetts.

Museum source ↗
06 / Preservation with a purpose.

A nonprofit museum

The museum identifies itself as a 501(c)(3) organization supporting preservation and education.
