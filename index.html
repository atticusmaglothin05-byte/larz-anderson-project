The project

How does a museum tell its story—and how do the people around it make that story their own?

This project explores the Larz Anderson Auto Museum through the language of its exhibits, the design of its spaces, and the conversations that bring automotive history to life.

Museum facts: Larz Anderson Auto Museum and its collection history.

01 / The settingHistory, preserved

More than a backdrop

The place is part
of the story.

The museum occupies an 1888 carriage house in Larz Anderson Park. Its historic architecture gives the collection a setting that is itself an object of study.

The museum describes the Anderson vehicles as “America’s Oldest Car Collection.” The collection began in 1899; tours of the carriage house were offered by 1927, and the museum opened in October 1949.
